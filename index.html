10-Minute Visual Reset

Use this brief visual-spatial activity as a post-incident reset. It is not therapy, crisis support, or medical treatment. If distress remains elevated, reach out — to peer support, a trusted supervisor or teammate, chaplain support, a close friend or partner, or a qualified mental health professional.

  • Take one slow breath before you begin.
  • Notice your feet, your hands, and your breathing.
  • Set your attention on the shapes, movement, color, and placement.
  • The goal is not performance. The goal is visual focus and nervous system downshifting.
